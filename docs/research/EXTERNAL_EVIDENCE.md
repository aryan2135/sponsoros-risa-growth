## (c) Recommendation: Bengaluru (provisional choice holds, with a caveat)

- **Densest student base in India:** Bengaluru Urban has more colleges than any other district in the country (1,148 in AISHE 2023-24, about 1.5x the #2 district, Jaipur), and it has held #1 in every AISHE edition checked (E1, E2). Karnataka's college density is roughly double the national average (69 vs 32 per lakh, E4).
- **Most youth-category sponsor HQs nearby:** Bengaluru has the largest cluster of the new-economy brands most likely to pay cash for Gen Z reach (fintech/UPI, D2C, quick-commerce, edtech). It holds 26 of India's 73 Hurun-listed unicorns, more than Delhi-NCR (12) and Mumbai (11) combined (E9, E10), and over 70% of Karnataka's 21,163 DPIIT startups (E7, E8).
- **Gen Z spend fits the sponsor categories:** Gen Z accounts for 43-48% of spending in dining, out-of-home entertainment and fashion (E11). These map to the QSR, beverage, D2C and entertainment sponsor ICP. Caveat: legacy FMCG/beverage CMOs and agencies sit mainly in Mumbai and NCR (E12), so the sales team needs a Mumbai/Delhi outreach plan. Delhi NCR is the strongest alternative if the ICP shifts to large ad-spend FMCG.

- **Delhi NCR's startup count understates the region:** the E7 Delhi figure excludes Gurugram (Haryana) and Noida (UP). The Tamil Nadu figure (13,780) was seen only in an embedded post.
- **Unicorn counts conflict by methodology:** Hurun 73 total vs Dealroom 110 vs a Karnataka-government claim of "57 of 131". None of these were fetched as primary reports.
